Meet Mr. Young

A little about me, how I teach, and what you can expect in our class.

MYMr. Young
Hello

Nice to meet you

Hello! I'm Mr. Young, and I'm glad you're here. I teach 7th Grade English Language Arts and Reading, and this page is a little about who I am, how I teach, and what you can expect in our class.

01

About me

I was born and raised in San Antonio, Texas, and this city will always feel like home. I care about building a classroom where students feel known, supported, and ready to grow.

02

Education

I am a graduate of The University of Texas at San Antonio with my B.A. in Education and a Concentration in English Language Arts and Social Studies. I am a member of the NISD family and completed my yearlong student teaching within the district.

03

How I teach

I start with relationships. When we have real rapport, students feel safe enough to try, ask questions, and grow as readers and writers. I also model first: I do, we do, you do. That means I show you how, we practice together, and then you try it on your own. I keep expectations clear and consistent, because every student can grow.

04

What to expect in our class

You can count on steady routines, so you always know how class starts and what comes next. We'll read together and on our own, write often, and talk through our ideas in class discussions. I'll be clear about what is due and how to do it well.
